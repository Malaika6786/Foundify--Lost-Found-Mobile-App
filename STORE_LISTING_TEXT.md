## Full description (max 4000 characters)

**Lost something? Found something? Foundify helps you get it back to the right hands.**

Foundify is a community-powered lost & found platform that connects people who've lost an item with people who've found one — fast, safely, and for free.

**📍 REPORT IN SECONDS**
Post a lost or found item with photos, a category, and a location in under a minute. Add a reward if you'd like to encourage a return.

**🔍 SEARCH & FILTER**
Browse nearby lost and found reports, filter by category, distance, and date, and quickly spot the item that matches what you're looking for.

**🎯 SMART MATCHING**
Foundify automatically checks new reports against existing ones and surfaces possible matches — so you don't have to scroll through every post yourself.

**💬 BUILT-IN MESSAGING**
Chat directly with the other person inside the app — no need to share your phone number unless you want to. Send photos, coordinate details, and keep the whole conversation in one place, tied to the exact item you're discussing.

**🔔 REAL-TIME NOTIFICATIONS**
Get notified the moment someone messages you, likes or comments on your post, a possible match is found, or a new item is reported nearby — so you're never the last to know.

**🤝 SAFE MEETUPS**
Coordinate a safe, public meetup spot to hand over or collect an item, with drop-off locations for extra peace of mind.

**🏷️ QR ITEM TAGS**
Attach a Foundify tag to your keys, bag, or valuables. If someone finds them, a quick scan lets them message you directly — even if they don't have the app installed yet.

**✅ MARK ITEMS RESOLVED**
Once you're reunited with your item (or you've returned one to its owner), mark it resolved and help keep the community feed relevant for everyone else.

**🔒 YOUR PRIVACY, YOUR CONTROL**
Your phone number stays private unless you choose to share it. All accounts are protected with secure authentication, and you're always in control of what you post — with the option to edit, resolve, or delete your reports and delete your account at any time.

Whether it's a lost phone, a missing wallet, a found set of keys, or a beloved pet, Foundify makes it easier for lost items to find their way home.

**Download Foundify today and help your community reunite people with what they've lost.**
