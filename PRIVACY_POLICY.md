# Foundify Privacy Policy

_Last updated: September 13, 2026_

This policy describes what Foundify ("the app," "we," "us") collects, why, and how it's used. Foundify helps people report and recover lost and found items.

## Information We Collect

**Account information**: email address, password (stored securely, hashed — we never see it in plain text), full name, username, and optionally your phone number and profile photo. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email, and profile picture from Google.

**Location**: when you report an item and choose "Use my location," we collect your device's GPS coordinates at that moment to attach a location to the report. We do not track your location in the background or at any other time.

**Photos**: images you upload when reporting a lost or found item, and your profile picture.

**Content you create**: item titles and descriptions, chat messages you send to other users, comments and likes on item posts, item tags you create, and meetup/drop-off locations you add.

**Device information**: a push-notification token tied to your account, used to deliver notifications (new matches, messages, item updates) to your device.

## How We Use This Information

- To operate the app's core features: posting and browsing lost/found items, matching lost reports against found reports, messaging between users, and notifications.
- To let other users contact you about an item you reported (via in-app message, and via phone/email only if you've added them to your profile).
- To send you push notifications about activity relevant to your reports.

## Sharing

We use the following third-party services to operate the app:
- **Supabase** — hosts our database, authentication, and file storage.
- **Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google)** — delivers push notifications to your device.
- **Google Sign-In** — optional alternative to signing up with email/password.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers.

## Your Choices

- **Phone number** is optional. If you don't add one, other users can only reach you through in-app messaging.
- **Notifications** can be turned off in Settings.
- **Account deletion**: you can permanently delete your account and your reports, tags, alerts, and notifications at any time from Profile → Delete Account. Note that messages you've sent to other users remain visible in their conversation history after your account is deleted, since deleting your account doesn't reach into another person's private conversation.

## Data Retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete your profile, item reports, tags, alert preferences, and notifications. Messages already sent to other users are not retroactively removed from their side.

## Children's Privacy

Foundify is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.
